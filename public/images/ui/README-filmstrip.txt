Collection hero film strip (optional asset swap)

1. Add your PNG as:  public/images/ui/collection-hero-film-perforation.png
2. In app/globals.css, find .collection-hero { --collection-hero-filmstrip-url: ... }
   Change the url() to point to your .png (or keep the bundled .svg).

The bar is decorative only (aria-hidden); it tiles horizontally along the bottom of the hero.
